Flagship development

AB Colonnade

DHA Phase 1 · Sector F · Japan Bypass Road

Take more than a decade of market exposure and channel it into a project with a distinct identity.

AB Colonnade is a contemporary mixed-use development bridging DHA Phase 1 with Bahria Town. Retail frontage sits on the lower floors and compact serviced apartments above, with a classical façade and illuminated architectural detailing. It is our statement of intent: from building homes for clients to building a development identity of our own.
